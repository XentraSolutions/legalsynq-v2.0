ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE

B11 is the deterministic confidence/policy-decision layer.

The intended chain is:

B07 Classification
-> B08 Extraction
-> B09 Normalization
-> B10 Matching / Duplicate Signals
-> B11 Policy Evaluation
-> B12 Human Review / later downstream processing

B11 answers:

“Given everything currently known about this Intake artifact, what processing disposition should the platform recommend?”

B11 MUST NOT:
- create Case records;
- create Lien records;
- update master data;
- approve legal validity;
- merge duplicates;
- send notifications;
- create Flow tasks;
- invoke downstream product adapters.

8. DISPOSITION SEMANTICS
==================================================

- all configured block/review conditions pass;
- required evidence meets policy;
- auto-acceptable is enabled.

REVIEW_REQUIRED:
- potentially processable but human review is required.

NO_MATCH:
- a required entity cannot be matched sufficiently.

CONFLICTED:
- hard contradictory evidence exists.

DUPLICATE:
- duplicate policy fires.

BLOCKED:
- processing cannot safely continue because of a hard technical/policy condition.

These dispositions are recommendations only.
