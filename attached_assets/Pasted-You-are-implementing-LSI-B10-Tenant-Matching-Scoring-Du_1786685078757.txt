ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE

B10 is tenant-scoped candidate discovery, deterministic scoring, ranking, and duplicate detection.

The intended chain is:

B09 Normalized Facts
-> candidate discovery
-> field-level comparison
-> score calculation
-> candidate ranking
-> duplicate signals
-> B11 Confidence & Policy Engine

B10 answers:

“Which existing tenant-owned records are plausible candidates, how strongly do they match, and does this intake appear to duplicate something already known?”

B10 MUST NOT answer:

“Should this candidate be automatically accepted, rejected, routed, or written downstream?”
