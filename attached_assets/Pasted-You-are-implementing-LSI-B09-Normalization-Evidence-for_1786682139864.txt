ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE

B09 is deterministic normalization and evidence preservation.

The intended chain is:

B08 ArtifactExtraction
-> ArtifactExtractedFact.RawValue
-> B09 deterministic normalization
-> normalized comparison-ready candidate
-> structural validation
-> preserved evidence
-> B10 matching

B09 answers:

“How should this extracted value be represented consistently for comparison, and what source evidence supports it?”

B09 MUST NOT answer:

“Which patient, provider, facility, case, lien, attorney, or other LegalSynq master record does this value match?”
