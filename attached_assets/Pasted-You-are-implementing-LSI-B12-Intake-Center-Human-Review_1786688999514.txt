ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE

B12 is a human adjudication layer over immutable B07-B11 intelligence.

The intended flow is:

B04/B05/B06 Source + Artifact
-> B07 Classification
-> B08 Extraction
-> B09 Normalization
-> B10 Matching / Duplicate Signals
-> B11 Policy Evaluation
-> B12 Human Review
-> B13 Approved Snapshot

B12 may create HUMAN review decisions/corrections.

B12 MUST NOT rewrite historical AI/deterministic intelligence outputs.
