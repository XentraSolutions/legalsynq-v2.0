B12 Effective Reviewed Projection
-> B13 Approved Snapshot
-> B13 Product-Neutral Adapter Framework

“What exact reviewed data was approved, and what stable contract can downstream product adapters consume safely?”

“How should SynqLien create a Case or Lien?”
