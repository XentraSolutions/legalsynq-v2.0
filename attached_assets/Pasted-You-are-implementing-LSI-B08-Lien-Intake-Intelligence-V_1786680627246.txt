ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE

B08 is extraction, not normalization, matching, or business decisioning.

The intended chain is:

Email / Manual Intake
-> IntakeArtifact
-> B07 Classification
-> B08 Lien Intelligence Extraction
-> B09 Normalization & Evidence
-> B10 Matching

B08 answers:

“What lien-intake information appears to be present in this artifact?”

B08 MUST NOT answer:

“Which LegalSynq patient, provider, facility, case, lien, or record does this belong to?”
